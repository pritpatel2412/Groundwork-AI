STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PROCUREMENT & EXPENSE APPROVALS
Document Version: 2.4
Effective Date: Q3 2026

1. Objective
This document outlines the operational process for submitting, approving, and auditing employee procurement requests within the enterprise ERP ecosystem.

2. Submission Rules
Employees must initiate all expense and purchase requests via the employee portal. Every request must include an itemized invoice, vendor tax identification number, and budget allocation category code.

3. Approval Thresholds & Workflow
Purchase requests with a total amount under $1,000 are automatically approved by the system upon submission, provided the requesting department has remaining discretionary budget.
Purchase requests equal to or exceeding $1,000 require formal digital sign-off from the designated Department Operations Manager before funds are disbursed.
For capital expenditures exceeding $10,000, secondary approval from the Corporate Finance Controller is mandatory.

4. Audit & Payment Settlement
Once approved, the Procurement Service dispatches a notification to Accounts Payable. All audit records, timestamped managerial approvals, and invoice attachments are stored immutably for seven years to satisfy regulatory compliance requirements.
