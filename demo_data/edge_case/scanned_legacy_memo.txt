SUBJECT: WAREHOUSE EXPENSE DISPATCH GUIDELINES

Due to legacy system constraints, warehouse supervisors are authorized to release emergency supplies up to $2,500 without prior headquarter authorization.
All emergency dispatches must be manually recorded in the physical logbook before electronic ledger reconciliation at the end of each work shift.
Submissions with missing supervisor signatures will be flagged for secondary compliance auditing.
